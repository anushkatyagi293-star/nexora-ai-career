What is this assessment?

This assessment is designed to help you reflect on the activities you enjoy, the way you approach tasks and the skills you may want to develop.

Answer all 10 questions honestly. There are no right or wrong answers. Your result will highlight a possible interest profile and suggest areas you can explore further.
