Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NEXORA AI and your career planning journey.

About NEXORA AI

What is NEXORA AI?

NEXORA AI is an independent career-planning platform designed to help people explore career options, understand their strengths and plan their next steps.

Who can use NEXORA AI?

Students, graduates, job seekers and professionals can use the platform to organize their career goals and explore learning opportunities.

Is NEXORA AI free to use?

The career planning tools currently available on the website are free to use.

Career Planning Tools

How does the career roadmap tool work?

Enter your career goal and relevant details to generate a structured starter roadmap with suggested steps and milestones.

What is the skills assessment?

The skills assessment is a short quiz that helps you reflect on your interests, strengths and possible areas for improvement.

Can I create a resume using NEXORA AI?

Yes. The resume builder helps you prepare a basic resume draft using the information you provide.

Does NEXORA AI provide live AI-generated advice?

The currently available tools use predefined templates and browser-based logic to provide structured guidance. They do not connect to a live AI service.

Privacy and Usage

Is my progress saved?

Some progress information is saved in your browser using local storage. Clearing browser data may remove saved information.

Can I use NEXORA AI on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Does NEXORA AI guarantee a job?

No. NEXORA AI is a career-planning and educational resource. It does not guarantee employment, admission or a particular career outcome.
